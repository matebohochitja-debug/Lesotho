Places to Visit in Lesotho

Here are some places Visitor might enjoy exploring

Thaba-Bosiu

Visitors can learn about Basotho history and culture here.

Katse dam

Katse Dam is surrounded by mountain scenery. Visitors can see the dam and enjoy activities such as boat tours.

Maletsunyana Falls

Maletsunyana Falls is a tall waterfall near Semonkong. Visitors can enjoy the views and explore the area.

Mohale Dam

Mohale Dam is another large dam in the mountains. Visitors can enjoy the scenery and learn about the dam

Bokong Nature Reserve

Bokong Nature Reserve is a natural area where visitors can enjoy and look for birds.

Thabana Ntlenyana

Thabana Ntlenyana is the highest mountain in Lesotho. Visitors can explore the highlands and enjoy the view.
