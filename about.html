Lesotho is a small country in Southern Africa,but because of its nature beauty is not considered that small. It is called Mountain Kingdom because it has many mountains as well as Waterfalls for instance "Maletsunyana Waterfall." Its capital city is Maseru.

The people of Lesotho are called Basotho. Lesotho has its own traditions and culture. the Basotho hat, called Mokorotlo is a well-known symbol of the country.

Lesotho has mountains, valleys and rivers as well as huge dams. Visitors can enjoy bird watching, the falling waters, pony ride, hiking and the lessons about Basotho and how they used to do for them to survive in the old days as well as what they used for hurnting.
